Waitless is a software solution for hospitals and clinics.
It merges:

1. A clinic management system, through which hospitals can:
    - Manage doctors and their schedule
    - Receptionists can book visits to walk-in patients.
    - other features...
- The main advantage of the system is the "Live Queue," which allows any patient to see how many patients are ahead of them.
- Users can access the queue page through a link, and they may also receive updates directly via WhatsApp as live notifications.

* The business model is "subscription" based. *

2. A booking marketplace (like Veezeta)
- you can add your clinic/hospital to the marketplace website so users can book visits online.
- In future, The booking system can be "Commision" based besides the SAS subscription.

Overall system edge:
    accurate availability
    accurate wait times
    fewer fake schedules
    better patient trust
